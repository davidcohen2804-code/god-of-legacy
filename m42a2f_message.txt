REGRESSIONS
All 177 unit tests pass — new among them: B's distance at 3, 7, 9 and 10.5 m apart, with his feet on the screen and the target off its bar; letting the target go eases the aim home without a jump. The full browser suite passes on 8318f00, all three profiles: Android Chromium and 16:9 Chromium (both required) and iPhone WebKit; nothing failed (the iPhone profile logged only WebKit's usual WebGL notice, "glBlitFramebuffer: read and write color attachments cannot be the same image", as in earlier WebKit runs). Its first run, on 021f496, failed through the harness and the test machine, not the game: that machine ran about 1.5× slower than usual, so the Android profile overran its 40-min limit and, not being stopped, ran on beside the next profiles; the 16:9 benchmark, at 12 s per preset and about 2.4 frames a second, saw no strike; and the iPhone's WebKit crashed while starting the engine ("RuntimeError: Out of bounds memory access"). The harness fix: a profile that runs out of time is now stopped (its browser closed) and reported with the steps it passed; the limit is 60 min; the benchmark plays 20 s per preset. That WebKit start-up crash stays open: 2 in 12 iPhone-profile runs so far (about 22 page loads), the same error at start-up each time, never once the game was running; David's iPhone will show whether real Safari has it.
